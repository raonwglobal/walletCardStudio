# Pricing and Packaging Hypotheses

These are hypotheses for customer discovery, not committed prices. Validate willingness to pay, costs and competitor positioning before launch.

## Free — acquisition and personal use

- 1–2 digital cards.
- Core templates, basic colors and on-device vCard QR.
- Local save and JSON export.
- No promise of cloud backup, team administration, analytics or real access control.

## Pro — individual productivity

Initial price hypothesis: **US$4.99–7.99/month**.

Potential paid value: more cards, premium templates, advanced branding, custom QR styling, cloud sync, export formats and basic engagement analytics. Keep privacy controls and basic portability available to all users.

## Team — organization management

Initial price hypothesis: **US$4.99–7.99 per active member/month**, with a minimum team size or monthly minimum tested separately.

Potential paid value: shared brand templates, locked design fields, member invitations, bulk onboarding, employee directory, centralized lifecycle management, organization analytics and priority support.

Avoid a flat low monthly price that accidentally includes unlimited support, infrastructure, SSO and access-control obligations.

## Enterprise — negotiated

Custom pricing based on seats, security/compliance needs, integrations, support and service levels. Potential scope: SSO/SCIM, HR integrations, audit export, retention controls, SLA and dedicated support.

Physical access-control integration must be separately scoped, threat-modeled and piloted. Do not bundle it as an already-working feature.

## Monetization principles

- Monetize workflow and team value, not only the number of cards.
- Preserve a useful free sharing loop.
- Make team billing per active seat or an explicitly defined seat band.
- Price integrations, high-volume provisioning and premium support against actual delivery cost.
- Consider AI design/background-removal credits only after measuring model cost, usage and conversion.

## Validation experiments

1. Interview 10–15 individual users and 5–10 teams about current workflow and switching cost.
2. Test Pro price points using a transparent landing-page or concierge pilot.
3. Run paid Team pilots before building the full admin platform.
4. Measure activation, card completion, share/export, D7/D30 retention, paid conversion, churn, support burden and gross margin.
5. Publish clear limits, cancellation terms and data export/deletion behavior before accepting payment.
